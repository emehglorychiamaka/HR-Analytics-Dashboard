# Project Summary

## Overview

Employee attrition is a major concern for organizations because it increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, and affects overall business performance. Understanding why employees leave enables Human Resource (HR) teams to implement targeted retention strategies and improve workforce stability.

This project analyzes employee data using Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Power BI to identify key factors influencing employee attrition. Through data profiling, cleaning, modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard development, the project transforms raw HR data into actionable business insights for decision-makers.

The dashboard provides executives and HR professionals with a centralized view of workforce demographics, employee satisfaction, compensation, career growth, overtime, and attrition trends to support evidence-based decision-making.

## Business Questions

The project answers the following business questions:

- Which departments experience the highest employee attrition?
- Which job roles have the highest turnover?
- Which age groups are most likely to leave?
- Does overtime contribute to employee attrition?
- Does salary influence employee retention?
- How satisfied are employees before leaving?
- Which workforce segments require retention strategies?
